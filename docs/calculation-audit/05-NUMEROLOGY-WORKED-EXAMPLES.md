# Numerology Source-Backed Worked Examples

These are transcriptions and arithmetic checks of examples in the cited
editions. They are evidence fixtures for future review, not implemented
calculations. Where a source omits a derivation or contains an inconsistency,
the gap is retained rather than repaired.

F/O/X=6, G/P/Y=7, H/Q/Z=8, I/R=9.

| Component | Letter total | Reduced component |
|---|---:|---:|

| Elder | 26 | 8 |

Name Vibration before its final reduction.

### January 17, 1872 - Birth Vibration (p. 19)

| Component | Source calculation | Value used |

### John Wanamaker - unresolved birth example (p. 90)

The scan gives born July 11, 1838; John = 2, Wanamaker = 6, full name = 8.

### D. Tarot Signature: John Milton (pp. 38-40)

The Hebraic values are multiplied by descending converse positions within
each name, and Christian and surnames are totalled separately.

| Name | Positional products | Subtotal |
|---|---|---:|
| John | J: `1 + 4 = 4` as printed/read; O: 7x3=21; H: 8x2=16; N: 5x1=5 | 46 |
| Milton | M: 4x6=24; I: 1x5=5; L: 3x4=12; T: 4x3=12; O: 7x2=14; N: 5x1=5 | 72 |

The source gives Sun sign 9 and degree 28. It extends the totals:
`4+6+7+2+9+2+8 = 38` (key number). It adds the birth year:
`38 + 1608 = 1646`, then identifies Tarot Keys 38 -> 11 and 1646 -> 17.

The first John operator is printed/read as `+` in the scan/OCR even though
the prose says to multiply; keep the line and reported subtotal as printed,
and record that notation as ambiguous.

For the 1674 example, p. 40 prints `38 + 1674 = 1712 = 13`. The digit sum
of 1712 is 11, not 13. Do not present 13 as a reproducible digit reduction;
it is the source's printed result and an arithmetic inconsistency.

### E. Table of Three: June 26, 1899 at 10 a.m. (pp. 41-44)

The source distinguishes the familiar numeric square on p. 41:

```text
4 9 2
3 5 7
8 1 6
```

from the planetary arrangement for the birth chart on p. 42:

```text
3 1 9
6 7 5
2 8 4
```

For the example, the source uses the noon preceding the 10 a.m. birth:
June 25, 1899. It omits century digits and places `25/6/99` in the chart.
The printed total is `25699`; digit extension gives
`2+5+6+9+9 = 31 -> 4`.

The evidence supports this example only; do not infer general time cutoffs
or use the chart as a DOB-frequency Lo Shu grid.
