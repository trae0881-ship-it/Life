# Known limitations

As of 2026-10-17 LIFE has a local birth-chart calculation workbench, not a complete production astrology product. Do not market this vertical slice as fully verified or as the full requested system.

- Registration/login, Argon2id hashing, opaque server-side sessions, role guards, initial-admin provisioning, reviewer approvals, and owner-scoped birth-profile/saved-chart APIs are implemented; PostgreSQL migration and persistent workflows have not been tested against a configured database.
- Rate limiting is process-local and not shared across multiple application instances.
- A bundled GeoNames `cities500` gazetteer now powers offline birthplace search and fills coordinates/timezone from selected records. It covers populated places in the dataset, not every village, neighborhood, or address; manual entry remains available. No PDF report renderer is included. The UI renders the D1 planetary table, D9/D10 comparison tables, and a selector for calculated D2–D60 placements.
- Railway config and Dockerfile exist, but Linux/Docker execution and native loading have not been verified.
- The Windows binding loads; the pinned package includes a Linux x64 prebuild copied into the image, but compatibility is unverified.
- LIFE is AGPL-3.0-or-later. Swiss Ephemeris file redistribution terms still need review and block public release.
- The regression suite includes an offline single-epoch JPL Horizons DE441 cross-system comparison for ten tropical planetary positions. This does not validate houses, ayanamsa, time scales, Vedic rules, broad date ranges, network isolation, security, or performance.
- Unknown birth time is never guessed. The current release blocks the chart calculation and explains that Lagna/houses/time-specific placements are unavailable; date-only planetary ranges are not implemented.
- Tropical and configured sidereal longitudes are checked for consistency. House systems implemented are Whole Sign, Placidus, Koch, and Equal; other systems are rejected.
- D9, D10 and Vimshottari Mahadasha/Antardasha/Pratyantardasha, the D2–D60 varga mappings, KP (Krishnamurti) star/sub/sub-sub lords and significators, sunrise-adjusted Vara, sunrise/sunset/moonrise/moonset, transits and sign-ingress events, planetary dignity (including combustion), Parashari aspects, and yogas/doshas follow documented rule conventions and internal boundary/interval tests, but lack independent classical reference fixtures. Panchanga Nakshatra/Pada use 27 equal sidereal segments and are boundary-tested, but have no independent reference fixture. KP uses its own Krishnamurti ayanamsa and Placidus cusps and is reported as unavailable (null) rather than substituting another house system near the polar circle. Rise/set inherits the UTC-as-UT1 approximation and the binding's default refraction.
- Sampled dates used Swiss files without fallback; exact asset limits and full continuity are not established.
- The personal Kundli assistant is deterministic and topic-routed, not an open-ended conversational model. It only summarizes available chart fields and the existing rule-based interpretation engine. Marriage, government-job, career-event, property and foreign-travel timing rules are not implemented; calculated Dasha intervals and transit ingresses do not predict those events. Technical evidence remains in source wording; explanations and all 38 popular question prompts are available in English, Hindi, and Hinglish. Unsupported prompts and missing calculation inputs are reported explicitly.
